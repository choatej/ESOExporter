# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ESOExporter is a small hobby project and we want it to stay friendly. In issues,
pull requests and discussions:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the work. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
  or publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block users
who do not follow these standards.

## Reporting

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or open a
private security advisory on this repository (Security tab) and mark it as a
conduct report.
